Our school held a Kindness Fair last week. The gym was noisy and full of people. My class made a poster about brave firefighters and safe habits. Everyone tried to stay fit by joining the relay race. My friend Amy is slim and lucky — she won first prize for the best poster! Some students told silly and funny jokes on stage, and everyone laughed. We also sold tasty cookies to raise money for poor families in our city. "I feel glad to help others," my teacher said. A rich businessman came and donated a lot of money. He said the simple act of giving makes people happy. My little brother was careless and spilled juice on the flat table, but nobody got mad. The tickets were not expensive at all, just one dollar each. The weather outside was somewhat dim and awful with rain, but it was a tiring, wonderful day full of fun!

Our Class Pet

Our class officially got a new pet hamster this week! We named him Peanut. "Can I hold him?" I asked. "Sure!" said my teacher. Peanut is very soft and really cute. At first, he ran away quickly and hid in his little house. "Maybe he is scared," my friend said. We waited quietly, and then Peanut came out again. "Is he still hiding?" someone asked. "No, he is not hiding anymore," I said. "Yes, he wants to play!" We took turns holding Peanut together. I fed him a piece of carrot too. Everyone was very happy. "Can we feed him again tomorrow?" I asked. "Sure, we can!" said my teacher.
